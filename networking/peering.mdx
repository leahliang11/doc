BGP (Border Gateway Protocol) peering allows your autonomous system to connect directly to Joy Services' network, **AS152565**, and exchange traffic without relying on intermediary transit providers. By establishing a peering relationship, both networks benefit from improved performance, reduced latency, and more efficient use of bandwidth — ultimately delivering a better experience for your end-users. This page covers everything you need to know about peering with Joy Services, including peering types, technical requirements, and how to submit a request.

## About Joy Services Peering

Joy Services operates under **ASN 152565** (Business type). Peering with Joy Services offers the following advantages:

- **Improved network performance** — Direct traffic exchange reduces the number of hops between networks.
- **Reduced latency** — Shorter, more efficient routes mean faster data delivery.
- **Enhanced efficiency** — Less reliance on transit providers reduces overhead and improves overall throughput.

You can verify and inspect AS152565 through the following external references:

- [DNSChecker ASN Lookup — AS152565](https://dnschecker.org/asn-whois-lookup.php?query=152565)
- [BGP.tools — AS152565](https://bgp.tools/as/152565)

## Peering Types

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Public Peering">
    **Public peering** takes place at Internet Exchange Points (IXPs), which are physical infrastructure locations where multiple networks interconnect and exchange traffic. 

    - Cost-effective and widely accessible
    - Allows Joy Services to peer with many networks simultaneously at a single location
    - Suitable for most peering use cases where direct private connectivity is not required

    Public peering at IXPs is the standard entry point for new peering relationships with Joy Services.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Private Peering">
    **Private peering** is a direct connection established between two networks, bypassing any shared IXP infrastructure entirely.

    - Dedicated, point-to-point connection for maximum performance
    - Bypasses the IXP fabric, reducing potential congestion points
    - Ideal for high-volume traffic between large networks where consistent throughput is critical

    Private peering is typically reserved for partners with significant and sustained traffic volumes exchanged with Joy Services.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Requirements

Before submitting a peering request, ensure your network meets the following requirements:

- An **official Autonomous System Number (ASN)** registered for your network gateway
- Properly configured **BGP routers** capable of establishing and maintaining a BGP session
- All applicable **technical and operational requirements** for the chosen peering type (public or private) are met, including route policy compliance and 24/7 NOC reachability where applicable

## How to Request Peering

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify Your Network Meets Requirements">
    Confirm your routers are properly configured for BGP, that your BGP setup is operational, and that you hold an official ASN registered to your organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare Your Network Information">
    Gather all relevant details you will need to include in your request:
    - Your ASN
    - Contact details (technical and NOC contacts)
    - Preferred peering locations or IXPs
    - Estimated traffic volumes and direction
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a Peering Request Email">
    Email your network information and peering request to **support@joycloud.services**. Include all details gathered in the previous step to help Joy Services evaluate your request efficiently.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Joy Services Reviews Your Request">
    The Joy Services network team will review your submission and respond with next steps, any additional requirements, or confirmation that your request has been approved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete BGP Configuration on Both Ends">
    Once approved, work with Joy Services to complete the BGP session configuration on both sides. This includes exchanging BGP session parameters, prefix filters, and any relevant routing policies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the Peering Connection">
    After configuration is complete, verify that the BGP session is established and active, and that traffic is flowing as expected across the peering link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Contact [support@joycloud.services](mailto:support@joycloud.services) to begin a peering request. Include your ASN, contact information, and any relevant details about your network to get started.
</Note>
