Joy Services is built on a robust network infrastructure designed to deliver consistent, high-performance connectivity for every workload. At the core of our network is **Equal Diversion Technology**, which intelligently distributes traffic across multiple paths to eliminate bottlenecks and ensure reliable uptime. Combined with **BGP peering** support via ASN 152565, Joy Services provides the building blocks for a fast, resilient cloud environment. This section covers how our network works, its key benefits, and how you can connect directly through peering.

## Equal Diversion Technology

Equal Diversion Technology is Joy Services' approach to intelligent traffic distribution. Rather than routing all traffic through a single path — which can become a bottleneck under load — our network spreads traffic evenly across multiple paths simultaneously. This keeps performance consistent even as demand increases.

Equal Diversion Technology is achieved through two core mechanisms:

- **Load Balancing** — Traffic is distributed evenly across available network paths using load balancers, ensuring no single path is overwhelmed and that all resources are utilized efficiently.
- **Network Optimization** — Network settings are continuously tuned to facilitate smooth, uninterrupted data flow, minimizing congestion and reducing the chance of packet loss or degraded throughput.

Together, these mechanisms ensure that your VPS workloads benefit from stable, predictable network performance at all times.

## BGP Peering

Joy Services supports BGP (Border Gateway Protocol) peering under **ASN 152565**, enabling direct network-to-network connections with other autonomous systems. Peering allows traffic between networks to travel more directly, reducing hops, lowering latency, and improving overall throughput compared to relying solely on transit providers.

Whether you are looking for public peering at an Internet Exchange Point (IXP) or a private direct connection for high-volume traffic, Joy Services can accommodate your requirements.

For full details on peering types, requirements, and how to submit a request, visit the [BGP Peering](/networking/peering) page.
