Whether you're spinning up a single development box or running a fleet of production servers, Joy Services provides the compute power, redundancy, and network reach you need.

## What You Can Do with Joy Services

Joy Services brings together everything your infrastructure needs under one roof:

- **Compute instances** — Launch VPS servers with up to 750 GB RAM and 200 CPU cores. Choose from Ubuntu and Windows Server images with one-click OS installation.
- **Redundant storage** — Configure RAID 0 for performance or RAID 10 for fault tolerance. Add and extend disks without rebuilding your instance.
- **Automated backups** — Enable 24-hour automatic backups with configurable retention periods, priced by disk size.
- **Network monitoring** — View real-time CPU, memory, and bandwidth graphs from your dashboard.
- **REST API** — Programmatically retrieve account stats, list machines, inspect runtime metrics, and change power states using `POST /api/connect/`.
- **BGP peering** — Connect your autonomous system directly to AS152565 for improved latency and throughput.

## Get Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [joy.services/register](https://joy.services/register/) and verify your email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch your first instance">
    Navigate to the **Instances** tab in your dashboard and click **Create Instance**. Pick your RAM, CPU, storage, and OS — your server is provisioned instantly after payment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect and configure">
    Use the credentials emailed to you (or reset them from the dashboard) to SSH or RDP into your instance. Configure it to match your workload.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Integrate via API">
    Generate an API key from your account settings and start automating with the [Joy Services REST API](/api/overview).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Need help choosing a plan? See the [Instance Plans](/instances/create) guide for a comparison of RAM, CPU, and storage tiers.
</Tip>
